MEET THE Brand

Pink Cakes is a premium bespoke confectionery and custom cake studio based in the UAE.

Their audience includes celebration planners and discerning sweet lovers looking for an aspirational, sensory-led indulgence rather than just a quick neighborhood bakery fix. They are in the industry to transform baked goods into emotional centerpieces and unforgettable memories.

What differentiates them in a competitive market is their evolution into a highly curated luxury brand; they deliver a cohesive, story-led aesthetic that elevates everyday occasions into high-end celebrations.

THE Design PROCESS

WHAT WE Delivered

  • Brand Strategy

    Defines the brand’s positioning, purpose, audience, and overall direction, creating a clear foundation for consistent communication and growth.

  • Logo Design

    Creates a distinctive visual symbol that represents the brand and makes it easily identifiable and memorable.

  • Color Palette

    Establishes a consistent set of colors that communicates the brand’s personality, creates emotional impact, and improves recognition.

  • Typography

    Defines the fonts and text styles used across the brand, ensuring readability, hierarchy, and a consistent visual language.

  • Business Cards

    Provides a professional and consistent brand touchpoint, making it easier for clients and customers to remember and connect with the brand.

  • Website

    Creates a digital platform that communicates the brand, showcases its offerings, and provides a clear and engaging user experience.

  • Social Media Templates

    Creates reusable designs that maintain visual consistency across posts while making content creation faster and more efficient.

  • Brochure

    Communicates key information about the brand, products, or services in a structured and visually engaging format for customers.

  • Presentation Deck

    Provides a professional visual framework for presenting ideas, services, or business information clearly and consistently.

  • Brand Guidelines

    Creates a clear reference for using the brand’s logo, colors, typography, imagery, and visual elements consistently across all touchpoints.
